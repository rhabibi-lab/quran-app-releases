# Shifa Quran

**An exceptionally beautiful, peaceful, fast, and accurate Quran reading and listening experience.**

Shifa Quran is an Android app by **Habibi Lab**, designed to make reading and listening to the Holy Quran peaceful, accessible, and convenient.

### 📲 Download Shifa Quran

**[Download Shifa Quran on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rhabibi.shifaquran)**

### ✨ Features

- 📖 Read the Holy Quran in beautiful Uthmani script.
- 🎧 Listen to Quran recitations by multiple reciters.
- 🔄 Follow the recitation with synchronised ayah highlighting.
- 🌍 Explore translations in multiple languages.
- 🔖 Save your favourite surahs and bookmarks.
- 📥 Download audio for offline listening.
- 🌙 Enjoy a calm, elegant Emerald & Gold interface.
